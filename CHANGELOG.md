## 2.0.0

- Synthetic-only normalized models and deterministic geometry engine.
- Conventional three-bedroom, irregular courtyard and gable-roof house fixtures.
- Explicit flat/gable roofs, wall-linked openings and model-derived cameras.
- Furniture editing, scoped layout persistence, import/export and placement diagnostics.
- Keyboard walking with floor boundaries and solid-geometry collision.
- Local pinned Three.js runtime with license and provenance.
- Geometry, mesh, navigation, layout and compiled-viewer tests.
